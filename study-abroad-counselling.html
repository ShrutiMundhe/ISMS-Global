FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers!

How do I choose the right university?
Our counsellors evaluate your academic background, budget, career goals, and location preference to match you with top universities.
Do you provide support after I reach abroad?
Yes, we offer complete post-arrival assistance including airport pickup guidance, accommodation support, and local orientation.
What is the process to apply?
Book a free counselling session, submit your academic documents, receive university offer letters, and apply for your student visa with our guidance.
