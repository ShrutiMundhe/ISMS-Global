About University of Oxford

The University of Oxford is a collegiate research university in Oxford, England. With a rich history dating back to the 11th century, Oxford is consistently ranked as one of the top universities in the world. It offers outstanding teaching, world-class research facilities, and a global community of scholars.

11th century – One of the world's oldest universities
Innovative teaching and learning
39 Colleges & 6 Permanent Private Halls
Opportunities for interdisciplinary research
World-class faculty and research excellence
State-of-the-art libraries and facilities
Strong global alumni network
Vibrant student life in a historic city

Admission Requirements

Academic Requirements

High school completion with excellent academic record. Specific subject requirements vary by course.

English Language Proficiency

IELTS Overall 7.0+ (with no band less than 6.5) or TOEFL iBT 100+.

Standardized Tests

Some courses may require SAT / ACT / GRE / GMAT. Requirements vary by program.

Documents Required

Academic transcripts, Statement of Purpose, Letters of Recommendation, CV, Passport copy.
