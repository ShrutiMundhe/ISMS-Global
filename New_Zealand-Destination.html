Eligibility Criteria

Academic Admission Requirements for New Zealand

Postgraduate (PG) Courses

To apply for a postgraduate program (Master's, PhD) at New Zealand universities:

  • Bachelor's degree in a related domain from a recognized university.
  • Minimum marks of 50–60% in your undergraduate degree.
  • GMAT or GRE scores (required for some Business/MBA programs).
  • Relevant internships or 1-3 years work experience is highly beneficial.
  • English test score (IELTS, PTE Academic, or TOEFL).

Undergraduate (UG) Courses

For high-school graduates looking to start a Bachelor's degree:

  • Completed higher secondary school education from a recognized board (CBSE, ICSE, State Boards).
  • Academic score of 55–60% or higher depending on the university cut-offs.
  • Proof of English proficiency (IELTS, PTE, or board exam waiver).

Student Accommodation Options

University Halls

Located on-campus, perfect for meeting friends. Includes your own bedroom and shared common spaces.

Homestays

Living with a local family with meals included. Offers local support when settling in.

Shared Flats (Flatting)

Renting a room in a shared house and splitting bills. The most affordable option for senior students.

Managed Student Accommodation (PBSA)

Privately-run student complexes with fully furnished rooms, gyms, and designated study spaces.

Part-Time Work Rules for International Students

Working while studying is a great way to support yourself, meet new people, and build local networks:

  • Work up to 20 hours per week during semester terms.
  • Work full-time (40 hours/week) during scheduled holidays/breaks.
  • Master's by research and PhD students have no limit on their working hours.
  • Must work as an employee only (no self-employment/contract work).
  • An IRD (tax) number is required, and taxes must be paid on all earnings.
