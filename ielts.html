OVERVIEW & TEST MODULES

What is the IELTS?

International English Language Testing System: A popular test that people take to see how good they are at English. This test checks how well you can use English to talk to people and understand what they say and do. You do things like listen to someone, write something down and talk to someone. The International English Language Testing System is used worldwide by universities, employers and governments to help people study, work or migrate to a new country.

There are two primary forms of IELTS:

IELTS Academic

IELTS Academic – for applicants seeking admission to university programs.

IELTS General Training

IELTS General Training – for employment, immigration, and vocational training.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Pre-requisites of IELTS

Key entry guidelines and prerequisites for candidates taking the IELTS test:

No Qualification Limit

There is no prerequisite for any educational qualifications.

Recommended Age

It is advised for candidates who are 16 years old or above.

Mandatory Passport

Passports are often mandatory for registration purposes.

No Prior English Certificate

No prior English proficiency certificate is required.

Select Test Module

Pick the appropriate kind of test i.e. Academic or General training depending on your need.

SUCCESS STRATEGIES

IELTS Test Preparatory Tips

Follow these practical study guidelines to achieve top band scores in your IELTS exam:

1

Understand Test Format

Make yourself familiar with the format of all four parts of the test.

2

Regular Mock Tests

Take tests on a regular basis.

3

Daily Grammar & Vocab

Improve your vocabulary and grammatical skills on an everyday basis.

4

Academic Reading

Read English newspapers, books, academic articles.

5

Listen to Audio & Podcasts

Listen to English podcasts, English news and lectures.

6

Regular Speaking Practice

Practice speaking in English regularly with your partner or mentor.

7

Hone Writing Skills

Improve your letter writing and essay writing skills. Recognize your weaknesses and work on them.

8

Time Management

Manage your time well in the test.

9

Authentic Practice Material

Go through the authentic practice material of the IELTS test before the test.

Remark: IELTS test exemption criteria differ from university to university and country to country. Always go through the exact admission requirements of your preferred university.
